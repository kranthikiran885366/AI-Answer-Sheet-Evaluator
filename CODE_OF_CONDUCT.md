# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free community.

- Be respectful, considerate, and collaborative
- Assume positive intent and provide constructive feedback
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Respect privacy and confidentiality

Enforcement: Maintainers may take appropriate action, including warnings or removal from the project spaces.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please open a confidential issue and provide context. We will review promptly.
