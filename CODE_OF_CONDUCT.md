# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, political belief, profession, race, religion, sex, sexual orientation, socioeconomic status, or technical background.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- **Being respectful**: Use welcoming and inclusive language
- **Being collaborative**: Work together constructively and share knowledge
- **Being empathetic**: Show understanding and compassion for others
- **Giving constructive feedback**: Provide helpful, actionable suggestions
- **Assuming good intent**: Presume positive intentions in others' communication
- **Acknowledging differences**: Respect diverse perspectives and experiences
- **Focusing on what unites us**: The desire to improve the project

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- **Harassment**: Unwelcome sexual advances, intimidation, stalking
- **Discrimination**: Exclusion or derogatory treatment based on protected characteristics
- **Abuse**: Verbal abuse, personal attacks, insults
- **Disruption**: Intentionally derailing discussions or disrupting collaboration
- **Privacy violations**: Publishing private information without consent
- **Retaliation**: Punishing someone for reporting violations
- **Trolling**: Deliberately inflammatory or off-topic comments
- **Spam**: Unwanted promotional content or repeated messages

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- All project spaces (GitHub, discussions, issues, code reviews)
- Community spaces (forums, chat, social media representing the project)
- Official events (conferences, meetups, webinars)
- Public representations of the project

This Code of Conduct does not apply to:
- Private communications unrelated to the project
- Third-party platforms or events
- Behavior outside project spaces

## Enforcement

### Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by:

1. **Opening a confidential issue** on GitHub (if minor)
2. **Emailing maintainers** directly (for serious violations)
3. **Contacting a community moderator** if available

When reporting, please include:
- Description of the incident
- Date and time (if applicable)
- Location (which channel/discussion)
- Involved parties (if comfortable sharing)
- Impact on you or others
- Any relevant context or screenshots

**Your report will be handled with discretion and confidentiality.**

### Investigation & Response

Upon receiving a report, the maintainers will:

1. **Acknowledge** receipt of your report within 48 hours
2. **Assess** the situation and gather additional information if needed
3. **Respond** within 1 week with preliminary findings
4. **Take action** as appropriate to the severity
5. **Follow up** to ensure the issue is resolved

### Consequences

Violations may result in:

| Severity | Examples | Consequence |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| Minor | First-time, unintentional violations | Warning and education |
| Moderate | Repeated violations or targeted behavior | Temporary suspension |
| Severe | Harassment, threats, discrimination | Permanent ban |

### Graduated Response

We believe in proportionate responses:

1. **Warning**: Private discussion about the behavior
2. **Temporary Action**: Temporary suspension (1 week - 1 month)
3. **Permanent Action**: Removal from community and ban from future participation

Appeals to enforcement decisions can be submitted in writing with additional context or evidence.

## Guidelines for Community Members

### When Disagreeing

- Disagree respectfully
- Attack ideas, not people
- Assume good intent
- Seek to understand different perspectives
- Accept that reasonable people can differ

### Supporting Others

- Be inclusive of newcomers
- Help others learn and grow
- Celebrate others' contributions
- Offer mentorship and support
- Create a safe space for questions

### Reporting Responsibly

- Report through proper channels (not publicly)
- Provide context and specifics
- Avoid escalating to social media
- Allow time for investigation
- Respect confidentiality

## Guidelines for Maintainers

Maintainers should:

- **Lead by example**: Model respectful behavior
- **Respond promptly**: Address violations quickly
- **Be fair**: Apply standards consistently
- **Communicate clearly**: Explain decisions and reasoning
- **Listen actively**: Consider all perspectives
- **Act professionally**: Maintain neutrality and confidentiality
- **Educate**: Help contributors understand expectations
- **Protect privacy**: Keep reports confidential

## Resources

### For Those Experiencing Issues

- **Immediate help**: Contact a maintainer or community moderator
- **External support**: Reach out to community member you trust
- **Documentation**: Review community guidelines and policies
- **Escalation**: If not resolved, seek external mediation

### For Community Learning

- **Communication**: Study good communication practices
- **Conflict resolution**: Learn de-escalation techniques
- **Inclusive language**: Review inclusive communication guides
- **Accessibility**: Ensure contributions are accessible to all
